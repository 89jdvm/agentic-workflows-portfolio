# Fundación IKIAM. Achuar Nation

> Executive Director of Fundación IKIAM for the Achuar Nation of Ecuador: ran the full grant cycle and raised over $70K for food sovereignty and territorial protection.

## The role

As Executive Director from 2020 to 2021, I ran the foundation's grant cycle end to end for the Achuar Nation, from proposal to execution.

## The work

Over $70,000 secured in international grants for socio-environmental and food-sovereignty projects, plus early-warning satellite work for territorial protection. Working directly for an Indigenous nation, not for an intermediary, is where I learned that governance comes before everything else.
