# Amazon Andes Fund. IMPAQTO Capital

> Research consultant on Phase Zero of the Amazon Andes Fund, IMPAQTO Capital's impact-investing vehicle backing processors, exporters, and ingredient companies across the Amazon and Andes.

## The engagement

Phase Zero pipeline research for IMPAQTO Capital's Amazon Andes Fund, an impact-investing fund focused on the Amazon and Andes bioeconomy. My role is research consultant supporting the early-stage pipeline study. My Phase Zero work began in June 2026, and its evidence base was handed over to the fund team in September 2026.

## What I bring to it

Ten years of field relationships across the Ecuadorian Amazon, and a working knowledge of both sides of the table: the enterprises on the ground, and the capital looking for them. This is the bridge in one engagement, finance meeting territory.

## How I ran it

The engagement lived in one working repository, built the same way as my other systems: research procedures written as step-by-step guides, Python tools for the repeatable steps, and a single-file command center the team could open offline to study the pipeline and prepare interviews. Every high-stakes claim carries its source, and a second pass checked those claims before anything reached the client. The final handover organised the evidence, the interview summaries and a guide to the folders in the client's own Drive, with every file move logged so it could be undone.

## On confidentiality

Engagement details are held in confidence. What is public: the fund, the firm, and my role. That is the line, and I keep it. A fund deciding who to trust with its pipeline is exactly the kind of client who needs to know I will keep theirs.
