# Amazon Andes Fund. IMPAQTO Capital

Engagement details are held in confidence. What is public: the fund, the firm, and my role. That is the line, and I keep it. A fund deciding who to trust with its pipeline is exactly the kind of client who needs to know I will keep theirs.
